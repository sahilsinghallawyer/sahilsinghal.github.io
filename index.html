Research Focus
IV
Enforcement Studies

Understanding how fraud is built, hidden, and prosecuted.

The research draws exclusively on public enforcement records case filings, deferred prosecution agreements, regulatory decisions, and court judgments. It examines how misconduct develops within institutional systems and why the same structural patterns recur across sectors and jurisdictions.

i

Corporate fraud architecture

How financial misconduct is structured the layering of transactions, concealment mechanisms, and gradual normalisation of irregular conduct.

ii

Enforcement case analysis

How regulatory agencies construct cases what evidence they treat as decisive and what distinguishes prosecuted cases from those that do not proceed.

iii

Control failure patterns

Why internal controls fail under real operational pressure how fraud exploits approval chains and the urgency created by performance targets.

iv

Genuine prevention vs. paper compliance

How courts and regulators distinguish real fraud prevention from documentation assembled after the fact.

v

Cross-jurisdiction enforcement trends

How enforcement priorities are evolving across India, the UK, and the US and what emerging patterns signal for compliance globally.
