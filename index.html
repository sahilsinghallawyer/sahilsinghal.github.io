About

Corporate fraud investigator.
Intelligence researcher.

With 8 years of operational experience as a Certified Fraud Examiner and Lawyer, I focus on understanding how corporate fraud and financial misconduct are constructed within organisations and how investigative processes and regulatory authorities reconstruct and evaluate those events.

My work combines forensic investigation methodology with legal reasoning. I do not simply identify what went wrong I analyse the institutional failures, documentation patterns, and behavioural indicators that allowed misconduct to develop and persist.

This approach means I understand not only how fraud happens, but how evidence of it is evaluated under judicial scrutiny a perspective that informs both investigative work at CSSG and the intelligence produced through Fraulex Intelligence.

Research Focus

Understanding how fraud is built,
hidden, and prosecuted.

My research examines the architecture of corporate fraud not just what happened, but how misconduct develops within institutional systems, why controls fail under real operational pressure, and how the same patterns recur across jurisdictions and sectors.

A significant focus is on enforcement behaviour how regulatory agencies like the DOJ, SFO, SFIO, and ED construct their cases, what documentary evidence they treat as decisive, and how the difference between genuine prevention and paper compliance is assessed under scrutiny.

This research informs both Fraulex intelligence products and enterprise advisory work ensuring that intelligence delivered to clients reflects how real investigations and enforcement proceedings actually develop.

Corporate Fraud Architecture
How financial misconduct is structured within organisations the layering, the concealment, the gradual normalisation of irregular behaviour.
Enforcement Case Analysis
DOJ, SFO, SFIO, FCA, and ED enforcement cases decoded for intelligence value what regulators found, what evidence was decisive.
Control Failure Patterns
Why internal controls fail under real operational pressure how fraud exploits approval chains, authority structures, and decision-making under urgency.
Legal Defensibility of Prevention
How courts and regulators distinguish genuine fraud prevention from paper compliance and what adequate procedures evidence actually requires.
Cross-Jurisdiction Enforcement Trends
How enforcement behaviour is evolving across India, UK, and US jurisdictions and what emerging patterns signal for compliance programmes globally.
